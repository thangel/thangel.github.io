Tidal turbine array modelling using goal-oriented mesh adaptation
-----------------------------------------------------------------

This study presents goal-oriented mesh adaptation for tidal-turbine array modelling. The method focuses computational resolution on the features most relevant to a modelling objective, supporting efficient and reliable simulation of tidal-energy arrays.
