Thetis-SWAN: A Python-interfaced wave-current interactions coupled system
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Research led by Anastasia Fragkou presents Thetis-SWAN, a Python-interfaced coupled modelling system for wave-current interactions. The work extends open coastal-ocean modelling workflows for applications where waves and currents need to be represented together.
