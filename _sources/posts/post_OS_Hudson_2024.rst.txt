Drivers of Laptev Sea interannual variability in salinity and temperature
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

This study, led by Phoebe Hudson, investigates the drivers of year-to-year variability in salinity and temperature in the Laptev Sea. The work contributes to understanding how physical processes shape conditions in Arctic shelf seas.
