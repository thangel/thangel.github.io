Iterative dynamics-based mesh discretisation for multi-scale coastal ocean modelling
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

New work led by Chris Old introduces an iterative, dynamics-based approach to mesh discretisation for multi-scale coastal-ocean modelling. The study examines how model resolution can be focused where it matters most for representing coastal dynamics, supporting more efficient and reliable simulations.
