A probabilistic approach to disentangling climate change and land-use change effects on river flows
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

This EGU General Assembly contribution presents a probabilistic approach for separating the effects of climate change and land-use change on river flows. It complements the group’s recent Journal of Hydrology article on the same broader research theme.
