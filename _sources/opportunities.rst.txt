PhD study
---------

Potential doctoral projects may involve coastal, offshore and estuarine engineering; environmental fluid mechanics; and computational methods for ocean-energy applications. A strong grounding in engineering hydraulics and an interest in programming, numerical methods and high-performance computing are valuable.

Before applying, please email `a.angeloudis@ed.ac.uk <mailto:a.angeloudis@ed.ac.uk>`_ with a short outline of your interests, background and proposed area of research. Supervision availability and funding vary, so an early discussion is helpful.

Research roles and collaboration
--------------------------------

Open postdoctoral, research and visiting opportunities are announced through the `Edinburgh Hydro-computation Group <https://uoe-hydrocomputation.github.io/people.html#opportunities>`_ and the University’s formal recruitment channels. Please get in touch if your interests align with the group’s `research themes <https://uoe-hydrocomputation.github.io/research.html>`_.
