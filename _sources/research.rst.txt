Research
========

.. raw:: html

   <p class="page-intro">I develop and apply computational models to understand environmental flows, support resilient infrastructure and enable responsible marine-energy development.</p>

My work is in environmental fluid mechanics, with a particular focus on coastal and ocean systems. I combine numerical modelling, observations, data analysis and optimisation to address questions that matter to infrastructure, ecosystems and the energy transition.

Research in E-COM
-----------------

I lead `E-COM — Edinburgh Coastal Ocean Modelling Group <https://uoe-hydrocomputation.github.io/>`_. Its website is the maintained home for current projects, researchers, software and collaborative work.

Focus areas
-----------

.. raw:: html

   <div class="list-grid">
     <div><h3>Marine energy and offshore systems</h3><p>Resource assessment, array design, operational control and environmental effects of tidal and other marine-energy technologies.</p></div>
     <div><h3>Coastal, estuarine and fluvial processes</h3><p>Hydrodynamics, mixing, sediment and water-quality processes in changing natural systems.</p></div>
     <div><h3>Numerical methods and software</h3><p>Reliable, scalable and reproducible modelling tools for scientific and engineering applications.</p></div>

Open-source software
--------------------

I use and contribute to open modelling tools that make research inspectable, reusable and extensible. `Thetis <https://thetisproject.org/>`_ is an open-source coastal-ocean model built on Firedrake and is a central component of much of this work. More software and workflow information is available on the `E-COM research page <https://uoe-hydrocomputation.github.io/research.html>`_.
