    .. container :: profileright
      
        Athanasios (Than) is a Reader in engineering fluid mechanics, incorporated within the Institute for Infrastructure and the Environment at the School of Engineering at the University of Edinburgh. He leads research on hydro-environmental engineering applications. His group, `E-COM — Edinburgh Coastal Ocean Modelling Group <https://uoe-hydrocomputation.github.io/>`_, coordinates research activities on computational methods for marine and offshore renewable energy, working closely with industry and supported by national and international research grants.
