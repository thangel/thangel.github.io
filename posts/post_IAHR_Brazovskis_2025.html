Sensitivity analysis of tidal stream resource using multi-scale channel network models

This IAHR World Congress paper applies multi-scale channel-network models to investigate the sensitivity of tidal-stream resource estimates. The work led by Emīls Brazovskis examines how model assumptions and channel-scale representation affect resource characterisation.
