Opportunities

I welcome enquiries from prospective PhD students, postdoctoral researchers and visiting researchers working in hydro-environmental engineering and computational fluid mechanics.

PhD study

Potential doctoral projects may involve coastal, offshore and estuarine engineering; environmental fluid mechanics; and computational methods for ocean-energy applications. A strong grounding in engineering hydraulics and an interest in programming, numerical methods and high-performance computing are valuable.

Before applying, please email a.angeloudis@ed.ac.uk with a short outline of your interests, background and proposed area of research. Supervision availability and funding vary, so an early discussion is helpful.

University application and funding

Start with the University of Edinburgh’s postgraduate application guidance. For funding information, see the University’s postgraduate funding overview, including guidance for UK and EU students and international students.

Research roles and collaboration

Open postdoctoral, research and visiting opportunities are announced through the Edinburgh Hydro-computation Group and the University’s formal recruitment channels. Please get in touch if your interests align with the group’s research themes.
